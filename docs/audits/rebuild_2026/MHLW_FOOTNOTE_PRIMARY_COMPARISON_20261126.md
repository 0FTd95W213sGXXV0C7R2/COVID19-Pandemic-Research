# MHLW primary PDF footnote comparison

Date: 2026-11-26 JST (time not verified).

Sources: MHLW, 2023-05-10 https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001143141.pdf notes 1–13; 2024-03-27 https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001238243.pdf notes 1–6. Both PDF texts directly inspected.

- Immediate beds: ready or already accepting patients at current phase/stage; 2024 note adds Hokkaido tertiary-region detail.
- Secured beds: capacity agreed with institutions for acceptance at some phase/stage.
- Occupancy: patients in secured beds divided by secured beds, times 100; separate general, severe and (in 2024) moderate II groups. Never substitute all inpatients or immediate beds as denominator.
- Welfare facility patients: May note 8 explicitly excludes people counted in inpatient or accommodation groups; March note 5 lacks that language. Hold harmonization pending review.
- May severe outside secured beds is section (1) ii-3; confirms previous candidate misclassification in representative PDF.
- May correction notes 9–13: May 26 Shiga/Hiroshima secured beds and Okinawa severe secured beds; June 15 Gunma severe immediate and secured; July 26 Osaka severe immediate and secured; Aug 16 Miyazaki inpatient split; Sep 6 Hiroshima secured beds. Original pre-revision values not verified.
- March dash semantics not defined explicitly in notes. Preserve tokens; no zero imputation. Hokkaido detail and flexible stage 1 operation require annotation.

Only two representative reports verified here, not all 171 or all schema groups. No final cross-period harmonization approved.
